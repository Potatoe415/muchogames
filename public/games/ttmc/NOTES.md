# TTMC

This is the TTMC game app.
To update questions, modify `public/data/ttmc/ttmc_data.json`.
The 2026-11-02 card photos have been transcribed (435 cards). 7 cards are missing because their answer side was not photographed (métiers, essuie-glaces, topinambours, demis, poupée Barbie, eaux gazeuses, serial killers). Challenge, "Hésite pas à gagner/débuter" and "C'est superbe" cards are not modelled in the data.
