# 0065 — Président: pile holds 2s on a quad or a hand-emptying play

Date: 2026-09-23
Status: Accepted
Decision: New `lib/client/usePresidentPileHold.ts` (`usePresidentPileHold` hook, `PILE_HOLD_MS = 2000`): once the server-confirmed pile shows a "big" play - a straight quad lead (4 cards of one rank in a single combo) or the exact combo that just emptied its seat's hand - `PresidentTable.tsx` keeps that combo on the felt for at least 2 seconds before letting any later play (a bot's move, or a fast opponent) replace it. `PresidentTable.tsx` feeds this hook the real `view.pile`/`view.finishedOrder.length` and only falls back to it once the local player's own optimistic play (`usePresidentOptimisticPlay`) has been confirmed - the local player's own tap still renders instantly, per the existing "instant local feel" contract, never delayed by this hold.
Context: User reported that in online games, a completed quad or a player's last card could get swept off the pile almost immediately by whoever/whatever moves next (often a bot, paced only by `botThinkMs`, default 800ms), so other real players never got a chance to actually see it. Asked for at least 2 seconds of guaranteed visibility, in the center of the table.
Rationale: A pure client-side display hold (rather than patching the server-authoritative turn/state machine, or the local-solo/ad-hoc bot loop's existing `didCollectTrick`/`COLLECT_DELAY_MS`) is the smallest change that actually guarantees visibility for every real viewer regardless of transport - solo, online, and ad-hoc all render through this same shared `PresidentTable.tsx`, so fixing it once here covers all three, including online where no equivalent pacing existed at all (`useBotRunner.ts` only awaits `botThinkMs` before a bot's *own* next move, nothing after a play lands). Implemented as a small pure state machine (`initPileHoldState`/`advancePileHold`/`endPileHold`, all unit-tested) driven by a `holdToken` counter rather than wall-clock timestamps, specifically to satisfy this project's `eslint-plugin-react-hooks` purity rules (no `Date.now()`/ref reads during render, no direct `setState` in a plain effect body) while still reliably re-arming the hold if a second big play gets queued immediately behind the first.
Consequences: New `lib/client/usePresidentPileHold.ts` + `usePresidentPileHold.test.ts` (13 tests). `PresidentTable.tsx`: 2-line hook call plus a one-line `displayPile` selection feeding `PileArea`, no other changes. No `GameState` shape change, no migration - purely a display-timing fix. Known trade-off (documented in the module's own comment): mounting/reconnecting directly onto an already-big pile does not retroactively get its own 2s window (kept simple to avoid an initial-render empty-pile flicker); a quad completed by matching up to 4 across several smaller plays (the existing burn mechanism, `usePileDisplay`'s `burning` flag, 1500ms) is intentionally left untouched rather than duplicated - only a straight quad lead or a hand-emptying combo triggers this new hold.
Alternatives_Rejected: Extending the local-solo/ad-hoc bot loop's `COLLECT_DELAY_MS`/`didCollectTrick` and adding an equivalent gate to `useBotRunner.ts` (would need three separate patches across driver files and still wouldn't cover a fast *human* opponent moving on server-side before other viewers' clients re-render; the client-side hold covers that case for free since it holds the display regardless of why/how fast the underlying state advanced); bumping the shared `.trick-collect-card` CSS/`BURN_ANIMATION_MS` duration to 2000ms (would also retime Coinche's/Bouilla's own trick-collect animation via the same shared class - out of scope, not requested).
