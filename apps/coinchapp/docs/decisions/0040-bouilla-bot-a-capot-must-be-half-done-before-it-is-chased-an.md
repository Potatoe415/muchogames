# 0040 — Bouilla bot: a Capot must be half-done before it is chased, and short suits are played to run dry

Date: 2026-08-03
Status: Accepted
Decision: Three changes to the Bouilla heuristic bot, all in `lib/bouilla/bot.ts` (+ one new helper in `rounds.ts`):
Context: User report on Bouilla only - "when they should not take the trick, the bots play their aces; they should give the lead away and build coupes sèches, so that when someone leads hearts and they have none, they can throw their dangerous card away". Root cause of the aces: `sweepAliveFor` is true after a single trick, and exactly one seat wins trick 1 of every round, so from trick 2 all four seats were in "win the trick" mode at once (the trick-1 winner chasing a Capot, the other three racing to break it). The 2026-07-17 vacuous-truth fix only covered the zero-trick case.
Rationale: Chasing a 13-trick sweep from one won trick is a lottery that costs real points on the way (and raises the round's total penalty from 65 to 195 when it lands), so "is this sweep actually half done" is the cheapest evidence test that keeps the existing Capot logic useful without it firing every round. Void-building is the standard Hearts/Barbu shape heuristic and the user's explicit ask; putting it *below* danger and below "shed the control card" keeps every previous safety rule intact and leaves the existing void-bait lead preference as the next tie-break. 200-deal bot-only simulation, before -> after: aces/kings played in tricks 1-3 dropped from 661/2400 to 75/2400 ("tricks") and 606/2400 to 88/2400 ("everything"); empty suits held after 6 tricks rose from 3.69 to 4.40 and 3.86 to 4.38 per deal.
Consequences: Bots now almost never complete a Capot in "tricks"/"everything" (they would have to win the first 7 tricks while actively ducking) - accepted, since the pre-change alternative was paying for a failed attempt in nearly every round. Three existing tests changed to keep their original intent under the new threshold (the two Capot tests now seed 7 won tricks via a new `allTricksWonBy` helper; the queen-grab test now seeds 2 collected queens). 8 new tests (130 total, was 122); typecheck clean, lint unchanged (only the pre-existing `useMatchStats.ts` error).
Alternatives_Rejected: Gating the Capot chase on "can this hand still win every remaining trick" instead of on progress (correct but far stricter - it would also have refused an almost-complete sweep like 11 tricks won with a 2 and a King left, which is exactly when chasing is right); inverting the existing "lead into the suit opponents are void in" preference, which is arguably a mistake of its own (a low lead into a suit two opponents are void in can win the trick and collect their discards) - left alone as out of scope for this report; a continuous "how likely is this lead to win" danger score (already rejected on 2026-07-17: it makes danger ties nearly impossible and silently kills every tie-break below it).
