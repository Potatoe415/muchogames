# 0075 - Sixth game: Belote, as a variant of the Coinche engine

Date: 2026-10-31
Status: Accepted
Decision: Add classic Belote as a new `GameType` `"belote"` that runs on the Coinche engine. `GameState.variant = "belote"` switches three things only: the deal (5 cards each plus a face-up `turnedCard`, the other 11 kept in a server-only `stock`), the bidding (first go-round takes the turned suit or passes, second names another suit or passes, eight passes redeal; the taker gets the turned card plus 2, the others 3), and the scoring (taker needs at least as many points as the defence, belote included; a tie scores each side its own points; failing gives the defence 162; capot 252; belote/rebelote stays with its holder). No tierce/cinquante/cent/carré announcements. Target points configurable (default 1000), bot level reused. Every mode: solo, online, ad-hoc.
Context: Owner request (2026-10-31): "en prenant exemple sur la coinche, dans coinchapp rajoute moi le jeu de la belote". Owner picked: engine variant, no announcements, configurable target, all modes, tie scores each side its own points, hub tile now.
Rationale: Trick rules, card points, belote/rebelote, dix de der, the table, the ISMCTS play bot and the online/local/ad-hoc plumbing are identical to Coinche; only the auction and the deal-result formula differ. Branching on `state.variant` inside `deal.ts`, `bidding.ts` (delegating to new `belote.ts`), `scoring.ts` and `bot.ts` (delegating to new `belote-bot.ts`) keeps one engine. `usesCoincheEngine(gameType)` covers the few server/UI checks that were `=== "coinche"`. The bidding UI is its own `BeloteBiddingPanel` (take/pass, then name a suit) beside the unchanged `BiddingPanel`; the turned card shows mid-table during bidding. Take bot (`decideBeloteTake`): trump points + length + side aces + belote, never without the trump Jack or 9; thresholds per punch calibrated over 3000 simulated deals (med takes 91% of deals and makes 86%). The ISMCTS determinizer pins the unplayed turned card to the taker's hand.
Consequences: `GameType` and `COINCHAPP_GAMES` gain `belote`, so coins, results and hub profile sync attribute it on its own. Belote takes go through the existing `placeBid`/`bid` paths as `{ type: "bid", suit }` (no value), so the instant-feel rule needs no new hook: the panel submits directly like Coinche's. Belote settings are only `targetPoints`, `botPunch`, `stillThereTimeoutSec`, `botThinkMs`. Solo saves under `coinchapp:local-belote:v1`. No splash art or hub thumbnail yet: `/belote` and its tiles use the plain felt background. No SQL change.
Alternatives_Rejected: A separate `lib/belote/` engine (would copy cards/trick/scoring/bot/redact and every client hook for the same trick game). Belote as a Coinche settings toggle under the `coinche` id (would mix both games in stats, coins and the hub catalog).
